# Project Discovery Brief — Expats.fi

_Status: complete · Last updated: 2026-08-03 · Approved for MVP execution by direct build instruction_

## 1. Project & goal
- Brand owner / approver: Kieran Donnelly.
- Project in one sentence: The useful front door for people who moved to Finland from another country.
- Why now: The domain and starter content exist, but the experience does not yet match the ambition.
- Primary outcome: Help visitors find an expat-owned business or a trustworthy starting point for a Finland-life question.
- What underperformed: The previous site had little navigation, weak presentation and only basic discovery tools.

## 2. Business & offer
- What it does: Free community business directory and editorial resource library.
- Offers: Business discovery; practical guides; free business submissions.
- Revenue / price model: Not defined for MVP. Listings are free.
- Primary conversion action: Use the directory or open a guide.
- Good vs bad lead: Genuine expat-owned Finland-based business vs irrelevant or unverifiable listing.

## 3. Audience & customer
- Primary audience: People planning a move to Finland or already living there after moving from abroad.
- B2C decision-maker: The visitor themselves.
- Core problem: Finnish systems and local options can be difficult to discover and understand in English.
- Awareness stage: Problem-aware and actively searching.
- Top objection: “Is this current, relevant and trustworthy?”
- Before → after: Uncertain and isolated → better informed and connected locally.

## 4. Competitors & market
- Alternatives: Search engines, Facebook groups, Reddit threads, fragmented official sites and word of mouth.
- Differentiator: Business discovery and practical settlement guidance in one focused Finland-wide destination.
- Category language: expat-owned businesses, life in Finland, permits, healthcare, transport, work.
- Words to avoid: lifestyle platitudes and unsupported claims of authority.

## 5. Pages & structure
- Priority pages: **Home**, **Business directory**, **Resource library**, business profiles, articles, list a business.
- Key actions: Search and open a listing; search and read a guide; suggest a business.
- Existing content: 3 businesses and 11 articles imported from the current site.
- Out of scope: Accounts, paid listings, reviews, events, forums, multilingual publishing and full CMS.

## 6. Brand & voice
- Feeling: A capable international neighbour who understands how Finnish life works.
- Voice: plain-spoken, warm, useful.
- Register: Product-led directory with an editorial brand surface.
- Existing assets: Existing business/article content; no dependable visual identity worth preserving.
- Visual direction: Finland-informed lake, snow, berry and summer-sun palette without tourism clichés.

## 7. Proof & assets
- Proof: Real listings and existing long-form articles.
- Asset gap: More businesses outside the capital region, original photography and named editorial contributors.
- Messaging constraints: General guidance is not legal advice; changing official rules must be verified at source.
- Target topics: expat businesses Finland, living in Finland, Finland residence permits, healthcare, transport and practical daily life.

## Synthesis
- Strongest angle: One place that connects practical help with the international people already building businesses in Finland.
- Biggest risk: A thin directory will not yet feel nationwide.
- Recommended positioning: “Find your people. Find your way.”
