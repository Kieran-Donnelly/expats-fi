## Verified details

- Kiasma, Friday 2 October, 10.00–20.00. Free admission all day. Free English public tour at 18.00; no advance registration, with sign-up at the information desk. Address: Mannerheiminaukio 2.
  - https://kiasma.fi/en/events/free-admission-day/
  - https://kiasma.fi/en/guided-tours/public-guided-tour-in-english/
- Korkeasaari Zoo, Monday 5 October, 10.00–16.00. Last entry 15.00. No advance booking; visitor numbers may be limited. The next free zoo day is Monday 2 November with the same hours and conditions.
  - https://korkeasaari.fi/en/visit-us/info/free-entry-days/
- Natural History Museum, Friday 9 October, 10.00–17.00. Free admission day. Visitors register at the ticket desk. Address: Pohjoinen Rautatiekatu 13.
  - https://www.helsinki.fi/en/luomus/visit-us/natural-history-museum
  - https://www.helsinki.fi/en/luomus/opening-hours
- HAM, Friday 30 October, 11.00–19.00. Free admission on the last Friday of the month. Address: Eteläinen Rautatiekatu 8.
  - https://www.hamhelsinki.fi/en/visitors/tickets-prices/
- Sinebrychoff Art Museum, Friday 30 October, 15.00–18.00. Free-admission evening. Address: Bulevardi 40.
  - https://sinebrychoffintaidemuseo.fi/en/other-events/ilmaisilta/
- Ateneum, Friday 6 November, 10.00–18.00. Free admission all day. Address: Kaivokatu 2.
  - https://ateneum.fi/en/other-events/ilmaispaiva/
- Kiasma, Friday 6 November, 10.00–20.00. Free admission all day. Address: Mannerheiminaukio 2.
  - https://kiasma.fi/en/events/free-admission-day/
- Natural History Museum, Wednesday 11 November, 10.00–17.00. Free admission for private visitors.
  - https://www.helsinki.fi/en/luomus/visit-us/natural-history-museum
- Helsinki City Museum is always free. Address: Aleksanterinkatu 16. Open Monday–Friday 11.00–19.00 and Saturday–Sunday 11.00–17.00.
- Tram Museum is always free. Address: Töölönkatu 51 A. Open daily 11.00–17.00.
- Worker’s Museum is free and open for the 2026 season through 1 November, Wednesday–Sunday 11.00–17.00. Address: Kirstinkuja 4.
  - https://www.helsinginkaupunginmuseo.fi/en/museums/
  - https://www.helsinginkaupunginmuseo.fi/en/museums/workers-museum/

## Caption

Free days worth sticking in the calendar this October and early November 👀

Kiasma is first up this Friday, with free entry all day and an English guided tour at 18.00. Then comes the big family one: Korkeasaari Zoo is completely free on Monday 5 October. There is no advance booking and visitor numbers may be limited, so getting there early is the sensible shout.

The Natural History Museum follows on 9 October, then HAM and Sinebrychoff both open their doors for free on 30 October.

We have added the strongest early-November dates too, including another free zoo Monday, Ateneum and Kiasma on the same Friday, and another Natural History Museum day.

We have also added three places you can visit for free without waiting for a special date: Helsinki City Museum, the Tram Museum and the Worker’s Museum, which stays open for the season through 1 November.

Save this for later, send it to somebody who is always looking for something to do and double-check the organiser’s page before heading out in case anything changes 🙌
