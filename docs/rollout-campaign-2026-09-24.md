# Expats.fi rollout campaign

Prepared: 24 September 2026

## The honest recommendation

Expats.fi is ready to be shared. The site is fast, technically sound, substantial enough to be useful and already appearing in both ordinary Google results and Google's AI search features.

The next risk is not that the site needs another section. It is that we launch once, post the same general introduction everywhere and then disappear. A useful rollout should give people several different reasons to visit over a few weeks.

Start with the story behind Expats.fi, then follow it with genuinely helpful pages for new arrivals, families, people looking for things to do and local business owners. That will show what the site actually does rather than asking people to care about a new website in the abstract.

## What success means

The first month is about learning, not chasing a giant follower number.

Watch for:

- people reaching a guide from social media and continuing to another page
- guide saves and shares
- business profile opens followed by website, booking or contact clicks
- new accounts followed by community posts or replies
- recurring visitors rather than one large launch-day spike
- real questions, corrections and suggestions that reveal what to improve next

Do not treat likes alone as success. A quiet post that sends ten people to the exact guide they needed is more valuable than a broad post that earns reactions but no useful action.

## Rollout order

### Day 1: introduce Expats.fi

Publish the main introduction on Instagram and the Expats.fi Facebook group. Pin it where the platform allows.

Use the branded Helsinki Cathedral artwork. Keep the caption focused on why the site exists, who it is for and what people can already find there.

### Day 3: prove it is useful

Share the Start Here route with one concrete message: arriving in Finland involves several systems, and this route helps people work through them in a sensible order.

### Day 5: show the local side

Share one visually strong Helsinki or Finland page. The playground map is ideal for family groups. The Explore hub or a timely local guide is better for a broad audience.

### Day 8: introduce the directory

Show three varied expat-owned or international-friendly businesses rather than posting a generic directory link on its own. Explain that listings are free and written properly, with owner approval where relevant.

### Day 11: invite participation

Introduce the moderated community board. Be clear that people can use a friendly anonymous public name, that new contributions are reviewed and that useful questions can shape future guides and events coverage.

### Weeks 3 and 4: establish a rhythm

Post two or three times per week, not every day for the sake of it:

- one practical guide
- one local place, family idea, event or business
- one question that invites useful community experience

Keep news for stories that materially affect international residents. Do not turn the social channels into an automated headline feed.

## Main launch copy

Hey everyone,

We are two Kiwi expats who have both spent years building lives here in Finland, and we have been quietly putting together something we wish had existed when we first arrived.

Most of the information you need is out there somewhere. The problem is that it is often spread across official websites, city pages, old Facebook posts and several tabs you promise yourself you will read later. Some of it is excellent, some of it has quietly gone out of date, and a lot of it does not make much sense until you already know how Finland works.

So we built Expats.fi.

It is a practical, plain-English guide to moving here, getting established and actually enjoying life once the paperwork calms down a bit. There are guides on permits, registration, work, housing, healthcare, studying and learning Finnish, alongside local events, family ideas, sport, food, day trips and places worth knowing around Helsinki and Finland.

We have also started building a directory of expat-owned and international-friendly businesses, plus a moderated community space where people can ask questions, share useful experience and help us spot the gaps we have missed.

The site is still growing, and that is part of the point. We want it to be shaped by the questions people are genuinely asking, not by what two blokes reckon everyone ought to know.

Have a look around, send it to somebody who might need it and please tell us what took you far too long to figure out when you arrived.

https://expats.fi/

Cheers legends,

The Expats.fi team

## Group-posting rules

- Read each group's promotion rules before posting.
- Adapt the opening to the group instead of pasting the same block everywhere.
- Lead with a page that solves a problem relevant to that group.
- Do not pretend to be an ordinary member who happened to discover the site. Say clearly that we built it.
- Do not post to many overlapping groups on the same day.
- Reply helpfully to comments without turning every answer into another link.
- Never use private-group conversations or member details as content without clear permission.

## Seven-day review

Compare each campaign link in Analytics:

1. sessions and engaged sessions by source and campaign content
2. guide opens, shares and saves
3. directory profile opens and outbound business actions
4. accounts created and community contributions
5. the most common landing pages and the next page people visit

Record useful comments and questions separately. Those are editorial signals, not analytics data.

## Twenty-eight-day review

Review Analytics and Search Console together:

1. Which social posts brought engaged visitors rather than brief clicks?
2. Which pages earned shares, saves or a useful next action?
3. Did branded searches for Expats.fi begin appearing?
4. Did any launch page gain impressions for new non-branded searches?
5. Where did people consistently stop before completing a useful action?
6. Which community questions deserve a guide, event listing or site improvement?

Strengthen the pages already proving useful before adding another major hub.
