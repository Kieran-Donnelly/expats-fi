# Social rollout watchlist

Public accounts and pages worth following for ideas, verification, community signals and possible future collaboration. This is a working editorial list, not permission to reuse anybody's images or copy.

## Rules for using the list

- Follow selectively and engage only where there is a genuine reason.
- Never reuse a business, creator or community member's image without permission.
- Verify event dates and practical claims against an organiser's official page before publishing.
- Treat community posts as leads, not confirmed facts.
- Keep comments useful and human. Do not drop unrelated Expats.fi links into somebody else's conversation.
- Do not collect or reproduce private-group content.
