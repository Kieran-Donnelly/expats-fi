# Expats.fi social visual style

Prepared: 30 September 2026

## The standard

Instagram posts should look designed by Expats.fi, not like a stock photograph with a caption added underneath. The first Helsinki Cathedral post is the visual benchmark: confident editorial typography, recognisable Finnish imagery, Expats.fi blue, off-white type and a few purposeful graphic details.

The goal is a recognisable family of posts, not one rigid template repeated forever.

## Core visual language

- Use the Expats.fi blue family as the anchor, with off-white, dark navy and small amounts of green or warm orange only when they help the subject.
- Use bold, oversized editorial type and short headlines that can be read on a phone without opening the caption.
- Add one or two recognisable brand devices, such as fine outline type, rings, the Finland outline, directional lines, a location label or a small numbered marker.
- Give every layout clear hierarchy: one headline, one supporting cue and one obvious Expats.fi signature.
- Use light texture, layering or cut-out treatment where it suits the image. Do not bury a good photograph under a heavy gradient.
- Keep the website and `@expats_fi` visible but secondary. They should sign the work rather than compete with the headline.
- Design inside safe margins so text is not clipped in the Instagram grid, feed or story preview.

## Formats

### Single-image post

Use for announcements, a strong guide, one timely event or one clear idea. The artwork must contain enough context to make sense before somebody reads the caption.

### Carousel

Use when the value is in several practical points. Slide one earns the swipe with a clear promise. Middle slides explain one point each. The final slide gives a useful next step, source or link-in-bio prompt.

### Story

Keep it lighter and more immediate, but still use the brand palette, type and a clear Expats.fi signature. Avoid shrinking feed artwork into a story canvas without redesigning it.

## Photography and permission

- Prefer Expats.fi-owned photography, licensed stock, public-domain or appropriately licensed official imagery.
- Directory photos and logos are approved for the relevant directory profile only unless the owner has clearly approved wider social use.
- Never lift a business member's supplied image for a standalone social post without recorded permission.
- When stock photography represents a general activity, avoid implying that it shows a named place, business, worker or facility.
- For guides about a real landmark or venue, use a genuine image of that place where licensing permits.

## Copy inside the artwork

- Keep it short, natural and plain-spoken.
- Do not paste the article title into the image if a tighter human headline works better.
- Avoid vague teaser language, forced jokes and clever-sounding lines that make the subject less clear.
- Never use an em dash.
- When an emoji ends a social sentence, it replaces the full stop rather than following one.

## Before publishing

1. Does this look recognisably like Expats.fi with the logo removed?
2. Can the main message be read at phone size?
3. Is the image licensed for this exact use?
4. Does it avoid implying that stock subjects are a named person, business or venue?
5. Is the image distinct from recent posts rather than another near-identical layout?
6. Does the caption add useful context instead of repeating the artwork?
7. Has the post been checked in the grid crop and full feed view?
