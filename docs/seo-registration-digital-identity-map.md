## Internal route

The registration, bank-account and Kela guides now include direct next-step links between this cluster and the Digital Finland guide. This gives readers a practical route through closely related tasks without making one enormous arrival guide.

## Source boundaries

- DVV owns registration in the Population Information System, personal identity codes, addresses and municipality-of-residence records.
- Banks own their customer checks, account services and the decision to issue strong identification credentials.
- Kela assesses benefit eligibility. A personal identity code or Finnish address does not by itself settle every entitlement question.
- The linked Finnish authority remains the final source for current rules and an individual's decision.
