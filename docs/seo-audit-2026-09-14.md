# Expats.fi rollout crawl

Checked: 14 September 2026  
Scope: every public URL in `https://expats.fi/sitemap.xml`, plus internal links found outside the sitemap

## Live baseline

- 498 sitemap URLs checked.
- Every sitemap URL returned HTTP 200 without redirecting.
- No sitemap URL was marked `noindex`.
- Every sitemap page had a unique title and meta description.
- Every sitemap page had a canonical URL and exactly one H1.
- Every canonical resolved to the same public page after normalising the preferred trailing slash.
- Every rendered image had an `alt` attribute.
- No broken visitor-facing internal link was found.

## Improvement made from this crawl

- Embassy and official-representation profiles now identify the mission as a `GovernmentOrganization` in structured data, alongside their existing breadcrumb data. Where available, the entity includes the mission name, country served, location, telephone, email and official website.
- The repeatable audit now requests the exact URL published in the sitemap. Its redirect and canonical checks compare equivalent URL forms consistently, so preferred trailing slashes are not misreported as faults.

## Content judgement

The shortest pages are embassy profiles. They are intentionally concise task pages containing unique mission, accreditation and contact information. Padding nearly 200 pages with generic country prose would make them less useful and risks creating low-value copy. The stronger move is accurate entity markup, reliable source links and regular data checks.

The guide, culture, family, study, housing and area sections are already substantial. Future expansion should be driven by real search demand, missing user journeys or fresh local usefulness, not a blanket word-count target.
