- `/resources/guide-moving-to-finland-quick-guide/` permanently redirects to `/start-here/first-90-days-in-finland/`.
- Both retired URLs are excluded from guide listings, search results and the sitemap. Their destinations remain indexable.

## Internal-link rules

- Use “moving to Finland checklist” when linking broadly to the Start Here hub.
- Use “your first 90 days in Finland” or “what to do after you arrive” for the post-arrival pathway.
- Link directly to a focused guide when the surrounding copy is already about DVV, permits, tax, customs or another specific task.
- Do not link every mention of moving to the same page. The anchor and destination should answer the reader's immediate question.

## Current source landscape

The strongest external results are official task-led checklists from InfoFinland and Suomi.fi, followed by individual authority pages from Migri, DVV, Vero and Kela. Expats.fi should not try to replace those decisions. Its advantage is the warm, ordered explanation of how the separate systems fit together, followed by direct links to the authority responsible for each step.
