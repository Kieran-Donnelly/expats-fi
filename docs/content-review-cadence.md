# Expats.fi content review cadence

## Purpose

Expats.fi should remain useful after publication. A guide that still reads well but contains an old rule, allowance, fee or authority link is not current enough.

## Review rhythm

- Review high-change guides every 90 days. This includes immigration and permits, work and money, housing, health and wellbeing, and family guidance.
- Review lower-change practical guides every 180 days.
- Review outdoor route and facility guides at least every 90 days and at the start of summer and winter. Recheck trail access, fire sites, toilets, transport and seasonal restrictions against the landowner or municipality.
- Check event details against the organiser before publication and remove ended events from active discovery automatically.
- Correct material changes as soon as they are discovered. The calendar is a backstop, not a reason to wait.

The admin dashboard shows published high-change guides whose `updatedAt` date is more than 90 days old. Saving a guide after a proper source review resets its place in that queue.

## What a review includes

1. Open every linked primary source and replace broken or superseded links.
2. Recheck eligibility rules, fees, dates, waiting periods, required documents and responsible authorities.
3. Remove claims that cannot be supported by a current official source.
4. Check that the introduction, headings and practical summary still match the current process.
5. Update the guide in Payload and publish the reviewed version.

For immigration, benefits, tax, healthcare, employment rights and housing law, prefer the responsible Finnish authority. Secondary reporting can help identify a change but should not be the final source for a rule.

## Immediate review triggers

Review affected guides outside the normal cycle when:

- Migri, Kela, Vero, Job Market Finland, a ministry, HSL or a city service announces a material rule or service change
- Search Console, a reader or a business reports a broken or misleading page
- an authority retires a service, form or URL
- a deadline, fee or eligibility threshold changes
- a news story materially changes advice given in an evergreen guide
