# Expats.fi community moderation

The goal is a board that feels useful, human and safe without turning Uriah and Kieran into full-time internet police. New members are reviewed first, trusted members can move more freely and automated checks only help sort the queue. A person still makes the final call.

## The five-minute routine

1. Open the Expats.fi admin dashboard.
2. Run the community review.
3. Read anything marked pending or flagged. Check the full context, not only the automated note.
4. Choose **Publish**, **Publish and trust**, or **Reject**.
5. Check the reports queue and either hide the content or dismiss the report.

While the board is small, checking once or twice a day is plenty. We should not promise round-the-clock moderation.

## Making the call

### Publish

Use this for an ordinary question, a genuine personal experience or a useful answer that follows the rules. It does not have to be beautifully written and the moderator does not have to agree with it.

### Publish and trust

Use this when a member has shown good judgement and made a clear, helpful contribution. Trust should come from how they behave, not whether their opinion matches ours. Trusted members can publish ordinary contributions without the new-member hold.

### Reject

Reject content that includes private personal data, threats, targeted harassment, impersonation, scams, dangerous instructions, repeated undisclosed promotion or material that clearly has nothing to do with living in or visiting Finland.

### Hide reported content

Hide content when a report points to a real safety or rules problem. Dismiss the report when the content can stay. A report is a request for a closer look, not proof that the person who posted did something wrong.

### Restrict a member

Use restriction for serious behaviour or a repeated pattern, not one awkwardly worded post. Keep the private moderation note short, factual and dated.

## Anonymous posts and replies

An anonymous contribution shows a friendly alias to the public. The account behind it remains visible to authorised moderators.

- Never reveal or hint at the person behind an alias in public.
- Do not try to infer their identity from their story.
- Do not copy their story into an article, event or social post without permission.
- Anonymity protects privacy. It does not protect harassment, scams or threats from moderation.

Only consider disclosing account information when there is a genuine legal or immediate safety requirement. Preserve the record and get appropriate professional or authority guidance rather than improvising.

## High-stakes posts

Personal experiences with Migri, Kela, healthcare, money or the law can be useful, but they must not be presented as guaranteed official advice. When approving a useful high-stakes thread, add or encourage a link to the current official source where practical.

If somebody appears to be in immediate danger, direct them to 112. The community board is not an emergency service and the team should not attempt to investigate an emergency through comments.

For a credible child-safety concern, do not publish identifying details. Preserve the private record and follow guidance from the appropriate Finnish authority.

## Turning good conversations into better Expats.fi content

Useful threads can be marked for an article, event, site improvement or follow-up. Look for repeated confusion, missing guides, changed official processes and local tips that deserve checking.

Use the conversation as a signal, then research the topic properly. Do not lift a member's wording, photographs or personal story without their clear permission.

## The tone we want

Keep decisions calm and plain-spoken. Explain what rule mattered when somebody needs an answer. There is no need to lecture people or make a small mistake feel like a courtroom drama.
