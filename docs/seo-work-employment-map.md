## Consolidated page

`/resources/guide-employment-te-employment-service/` redirects to the public employment-services guide. The old TE-office wording no longer deserves a separate page now that employment services are organised locally and Job Market Finland is the national online doorway.

## Internal route

The retained pages now use direct next-step links. Jobseekers move from search to qualifications, services and market context. People with an offer move from contracts to pay, unions and dispute help. The employment-services guide also separates registering as a jobseeker from a later Kela benefit decision.

## Source boundaries

- Job Market Finland and the relevant local employment authority own jobseeker registration and public employment services.
- The Finnish National Agency for Education or another competent authority decides recognition only where a formal decision is required. Many employers assess foreign qualifications themselves.
- The Occupational Safety and Health Administration provides official guidance on employment terms and working conditions.
- A union, unemployment fund, authority and lawyer have different roles. The correct route depends on the question and the person's membership or circumstances.
